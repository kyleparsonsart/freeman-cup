# The Freeman Cup: end-to-end audit

Sep 6, 2026, at HEAD `43da994`. Read against the code, not from memory: two reviewers walked the player/scorer paths and the commissioner/backend paths file by file; the public site was reviewed by its author. Line references are to `src/` unless noted.

Severity means one thing here: **what happens on the course if this is left alone.**

- **Blocker**: a real chance of a lost score, a locked-out player, or a wrong result on Oct 8 to 10.
- **Before Oct 8**: will bite at least once during the trip; fixable inside the freeze or by procedure.
- **Nice**: worth doing if the week has room.
- **2027**: real, but not this year.

## Off-season and pre-trip (now to Sep 27)

**Setup still lives in the SQL editor.** Emails, handicap indexes, names, tee times, and courses have no UI (`SettingsSheet.tsx` writes only round state, scorer, pairings, odds). Kyle needs the statements ready: `update player set email, handicap_index where name = ...` and `update tee_group set tee_time = ...`. A per-player handicap and email input on the Seats tab plus a tee-time input on each Scorers row is about an hour and freeze-safe; otherwise keep a runbook. **Before Oct 8.**

**Handicaps must be frozen before R1 goes live.** Results are stored per hole and only re-derived on a tap (`useEventData.ts:328`, `ScoringScreen.tsx:424`). Change an index Thursday night and Thursday's holes keep old results while the display shows new nets. Rule, not code. **Before Oct 8.**

**Pairings accept the same player twice.** No uniqueness check across slots or matches (`SettingsSheet.tsx:380-390`), and the desk counts the round ready anyway (`desk.ts:57-58`). Griffin in both slots collapses to a one-man side. Reject duplicates in `setSide`; require 8 distinct players at singles. **Before Oct 8.**

**Player swapped in at the last minute** is SQL plus a rule: update the existing row's name/email/handicap in place (keeps match references), `clear_seat()` if bound, add the auth user, resend the invite. The engine keys players by lowercase first name (`useEventData.ts:252`), so a substitute sharing a first name with anyone merges scores with them. Use a nickname in `player.name`. Write this down. **Before Oct 8.**

**Sign-in on the tee is the risk, not sign-in at home.** Supabase's OTP has a 60 s per-address cooldown and an hourly email cap; eight players plus retries on the first tee is close. The week-out email should say "install and sign in this week"; sessions don't expire. Check Authentication → Rate Limits before Oct 1. **Before Oct 8.**

**No backup beyond the plan default.** `match_hole_history` lives in the same database. A nightly `select public_scoreboard();` copied off Supabase is a complete score snapshot in one line. Do it each evening and before `reset_event`. **Before Oct 8.**

## Invites and comms (Sep 27 to Oct 7)

**Only the sign-in code email is wired.** Invitation HTML exists with `[Teammate]` placeholders still in it and depends on a Resend audience with first names. The pairings, day recap, finale, and week-out emails described in the carryover are not in the repo, and there is no Edge Function or Resend call anywhere in `src/`. Do not build one inside the freeze. Pairings go out as a text; a "Copy tomorrow's pairings" button on the desk (plain text: match, group, tee, scorer) is 30 minutes and is what you'll want at 10 pm. Recap and finale already exist in-app as moments. **Before Oct 8: decide; 2027: build.**

**Safari vs installed app.** The invite says add to home screen, but the app never detects `display-mode: standalone`. A player who signs in from the email link in Safari, then installs, is asked to sign in again inside the app with no explanation. Several will read that as "it didn't work". A one-card hint on the sign-in screen when not standalone on iOS, plus `<meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-title">` so the icon isn't labeled from the truncated `<title>`. **Before Oct 8.**

**Code screen has no "Send a new code."** Only "Use a different email" (`SignInScreen.tsx:129-134`); the cooldown error surfaces raw. Add the button with a 60 s countdown and a spam/Promotions hint naming `no-reply@thefreemancup.com`. **Before Oct 8.**

**"No seat for this email" is the wrong message for the likely case.** `claim_seat()` only binds rows with `auth_uid` null. Devin's and Matt's rows are bound to test accounts, and any transient failure lands on the same screen, which says the email isn't on the roster and offers only Sign out. Two copies (not on roster vs. seat already bound) and a Try again. Unbind Devin and Matt before invites. **Before Oct 8.**

## On the course

### Scorekeeper

**Submit races the last score** (short list #8). `pending` is set after a 2.5 s debounce; Submit is enabled in the gap; `submit_card()` can land before the upsert; RLS then refuses the queued row. Await `flushQueue()` and refuse if any row for the group remains; add a 10 s abort signal to the upsert so a hung fetch doesn't pin "Syncing 1…" forever.

**Byes make the card un-submittable.** Submit requires every hole filled (`card.ts:41`), and the override strip is hidden on bye holes. A 6&5 win that walks in leaves "5 holes still open" and no move except calling Kyle. The rule says everyone holes out; the fallback should exist anyway: allow Submit once every counting hole has a result and say "3 byes not played, card short for MVP" in the drawer. **Before Oct 8.**

**Fixing an old hole is one tap per hole to get back.** Correct hole 5 while on 14 and the pin sticks at 5; nine taps forward. When the pinned hole is behind the frontier, make the advance button "Back to hole 14 ›". **Before Oct 8.**

**Clearing a hand-set override leaves the hole in limbo.** Writes `result: null, derived: false` without re-deriving (`ScoringScreen.tsx:431`); with four scores present it shows "Not posted" until a score is toggled off and on. Call `settle()` on clear. **Before Oct 8.**

**"Waiting on Phil" never says what to do.** A partner picks up (against the rules, but it will happen) and the scorer stalls on hole 1. Append: "Enter what he'd have made, or tap Vikes / Halved / Celts above to set the hole by hand." **Before Oct 8.**

**Blocked-scores copy misses the handoff case.** The third and likeliest cause is a pencil handoff while the old scorer was offline; his rows come back with a raw RLS string. Add the line and map the message. **Nice.**

**Triple bogey needs a swipe.** The score row starts at eagle and shows five cells, so on a par 4 the window is 2 to 6 and every 7 needs the scroll gesture the tap guard exists to defuse. For 6 to 15 handicaps, start at par minus one. **Nice.**

**Offline scorer overwrites a commissioner correction.** The queue upserts unconditionally; a queued 5 re-lands over Kyle's clubhouse 4 when the group reconnects. Practical fix is a trigger that refuses when the server row is newer than what the phone saw, surfacing as a blocked row (the queue already shows those). **Nice; 2027 if not.**

### Player (not scoring)

**Foreground refetch** (short list #3) is the one that matters. Without it, a non-scorer's view is as fresh as the last event that happened to arrive while the app was open.

**Realtime on `match_hole`** (short list #2) is the other half. Verify in the two-phone test: enter a hole on phone A, watch phone B update without touching it.

**MVP board strikes through the trailing group during play.** Eligibility is `holes === max`, so the group that teed off ten minutes later is "card short" all round. Compute per completed round. **Nice.**

**MVP net uses match-context strokes** (relative to the low man in that match), so the race is partly a pairings lottery: Phil gets five shots against Brian and none against Kyle. Not a bug; a rule to write into the "Rules & Scoring" sheet, or switch the board to full course handicap. **Nice.**

**Sign out is one tap, no confirm, and leaves the queue.** On the course that's email + code + signal to get back; on a shared phone, unsynced rows flush under the next account. Confirm, refuse with a non-empty queue, clear the snapshot on sign out. **Before Oct 8.**

**"Good night" at 7 am.** The day recap auto-opens on the next cold open, usually the next morning when someone wants a tee time; the dismiss reads "Good night". "On to today". **Nice.**

### Captains

**Captains have no powers and no view beyond a player**, which is fine for 2026 with one thing to settle now: the shootout is entered commissioner-only and Kyle is a duelist. Either Griffin watches Kyle type, or `set_shootout` accepts `is_captain`. Also: the second commissioner (short list #7) could simply be the other captain. **Before Oct 8, decide.**

### Commissioner

**Acting-as stays armed** across reopens (`view.ts:115-127`). Kyle arms it Thursday morning to go live and the scores-anywhere power is on all week while he's also scoring his own group. Consider auto-disarm when his own card is open. **Nice.**

**After "Lock it anyway", Reopen is three screens away.** Put Reopen beside each card-in row on the desk. **Nice.**

**Supabase down mid-round**: scoring survives (queue, snapshot), everything administrative doesn't (go-live, switch, submit, reopen, shootout show raw errors). Runbook line: play on, keep scoring, hand cards in later. **Document.**

## End of trip

**A false clinch burns the finale.** Finals are walked by `updated_at`; a fat-fingered hole that closes a match early can trigger `won` on every phone, and the finale is keyed as the literal string `'won'` in seen-state, so after the correction the real clinch won't auto-open on any phone that saw the false one. Key it by winner and score, or clear it when `won` returns to null. **Nice, but cheap.**

**After Oct 10 the data sits live.** "Clear all scores" is one confirmation away all winter; a 2027 tester wipes 2026 (history survives). Set `event.previous_winner` and `previous_year` after the cup (the public site reads them), export the JSON, and consider revoking `reset_event` from `authenticated` until next September. **After the trip.**

**There is no event/year model.** `fetchTables` reads every table unfiltered and takes `events[0]`; `player.auth_uid` is globally unique so a returning player can't claim a 2027 seat while his 2026 row holds it; `reset_event` wipes every year; team names and trophy are hard-coded in the engine. Inserting a 2027 event row today would give eight rounds on Schedule and a clinch computed across both years. Decision to write into HANDOFF now: either a fresh Supabase project per year (cheapest; history is the export plus `previous_winner`), or a migration adding `event_id` scoping everywhere. **2027, but decide before anyone adds a row.**

## Patrons (thefreemancup.com)

**Share preview.** Sharing the link in iMessage or a group chat shows nothing: no Open Graph tags, no image. This is the page's entire distribution channel. Add `og:title`, `og:description`, and a 1200×630 brand PNG. **Before Oct 8, 20 min.**

**Staleness is invisible.** If the RPC starts failing after the first load, the page keeps showing old scores with the Live pill on. Show "Updated 9:41" next to the pill and dim it if the last fetch is older than three minutes. **Before Oct 8, 20 min.**

**Weather failure reads as "not yet".** If Open-Meteo is unreachable during the trip, the strip shows the placeholder cells and "appears seven days out", which is wrong on Thursday. Distinguish fetch failure from horizon. **Nice.**

**Final-state accordions all closed.** Day blocks open when live or next; after Saturday none are open, so the record page hides its results one tap deep. Open the last round when the phase is final. **Nice.**

**Keyboard and screen reader.** The accordion checkboxes are `display:none`, so they're unfocusable; the labels can't be reached by keyboard or announced. Use a visually-hidden class instead. **Nice.**

**The RPC publishes handicap indexes** (needed for stroke marks) and first names, unthrottled, to anyone. Eight friends; make sure they know before Sep 27. A 30 s edge cache in front of the RPC is the mitigation if it ever matters. **Document.**

**Off-season.** After Oct 10 the page shows "The final day" and the record until a 2027 event exists, which ties to the event-model decision above. The holder line will read correctly the moment `previous_winner` is set. **After the trip.**

**No analytics.** You won't know if anyone looked. Vercel Analytics is one line if you want to know. **Nice.**

## Security, briefly

Beyond the `player` policy (short list #1): the `handoff` policy on `tee_group` is `with check (true)` over all columns, so any group member can un-submit a signed card or change tee times via REST; fix with column-level grants (only `scorer_player_id` writable by `authenticated`; the rest goes through definer RPCs). `entered_by` and the history's `changed_by` are client-supplied, so the argument-settling audit trail can be forged by whoever holds the pencil; stamp both from `me()` in the trigger. Definer functions lack `set search_path = public` (advisor warning; low risk on Supabase). None of this matters among eight friends unless one of them is bored; the `player` one is the exception because it's accidental as well as deliberate.
